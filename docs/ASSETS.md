## Transparentni logo

[logo-transparent.png](../public/images/logo-transparent.png) je verzija logotipa sa alfa kanalom, korišćena u zaglavlju i footer-u. Pripremljena je ugrađenim ImageGen alatom na osnovu dostavljenog `logo.jpg`, uz uklanjanje bele pozadine. Transparentni spoljašnji razmak je skraćen i rezultat smešten u proporcije 349:93, u rezoluciji 1047 × 279 px. Vizuelno je upoređen na krem i tamnoj podlozi. Izvorni JPG je sačuvan.

Korišćen prompt:

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied existing Panoramica logo image. Remove ONLY its white / near-white background and make it genuinely transparent. Preserve the entire original logo exactly, including the Eco-Schools tree/book emblem on the left, the script Panoramica wordmark, the tiny slogan 'Lepši pogled na detinjstvo', and the Ferris wheel with children on the right. Keep all original colors, outlines, typography, spacing, fine details, composition and wide 349:93 proportions. No redesign, no added elements, no cropping, no replacement text, no shadows or new backdrop. This is faithful background extraction for the website's header and footer, not a logo recreation. Transparent alpha background.
